💬 How the app works
Tap any button — the app speaks the word out loud. Build sentences by tapping multiple buttons — the green bar at the top builds up your words. Tap ▶ Speak to say the full sentence. Tap ✕ to clear.
🔝 The 5 top buttons FREE
🧍 I AM — how I'm feeling right now
🙋 I WANT — something I need or want
✅ YES — agree
❌ NO — disagree or refuse
🆘 HELP ME — see Emergency section below
🆘 HELP ME button FREE
Quick tap → speaks "I need help" loudly + screen flashes red
Hold 2 seconds → opens the full red Emergency Screen

Emergency screen has 6 big buttons:
🆘 I Need Help · 🤕 I Am Hurt · 🏥 I Need Doctor
🚨 I Am Lost · 💧 I Need Water · 📞 Call My Carer

Call My Carer dials automatically on phones (iPhone & Android).
⚠️ Tablets & iPads: cannot make calls — the number is shown on screen so someone nearby can call.

Set up: Settings → Emergency Contact → enter carer's number.
📂 4 categories FREE
Swipe left/right or tap the tabs:
😊 Feelings · 🍎 Needs · 🏠 Places · 🤝 Social

35 built-in buttons per category (140 total). That's a lot — carers should hide buttons the child doesn't need yet (Settings → Edit layout). The child then only sees what's relevant. Custom buttons appear right after the built-ins — no gap. Use 🔍 Search any time to find a button fast.
🔍 Search FREE
Tap 🔍 in the top bar. Type any word — results appear instantly from all 140 built-in buttons plus your custom ones. Tap ▶ Speak to hear it, or tap the result to jump to that category.
🧘 Calm Breathing FREE
Tap 🧘 in the top bar. A circle expands and shrinks — child follows it to calm down.

Breathe in 4s → Hold 4s → Breathe out 6s

Great for anxiety, meltdowns or overwhelm.
⏱️ Visual Timer FREE
Tap ⏱️ in the top bar. Choose 1–30 minutes. A green ring counts down — turns orange then red as time runs out. Says "Time is up!" when finished.

Great for transitions: "5 more minutes then bath time."
🔊 Voice & Volume FREE
Settings → Voice → tap 👩 Female or 👨 Male → tap ▶ Test.

For the clearest voice on iPhone/iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English → download Serena (female) or Daniel (male).
On Android: Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech → install Google Text-to-Speech for best quality.

Also turn up your device volume using the physical buttons on the side of your device.
🌙 Dark mode & Display FREE
Dark mode — easier on eyes at night or for light-sensitive users.
Adult mode — deep teal theme for teens and adults.
High contrast — black background with vivid borders for maximum visibility.
Button size — Small / Normal / Large. Large mode always shows at least 3 columns — scroll to see all buttons.
👤 Profiles FREE — 1 profile
Settings → Profiles. Each profile has its own custom buttons and settings.

Free: 1 profile
Premium: 2 profiles
Premium Plus: unlimited profiles

Great for families where more than one child uses the same device.
🛠️ Setting up for your child RECOMMENDED
Aidova has 140 built-in buttons — more than most children need. The best experience comes from curating the app for your child:

Step 1 — Hide unused buttons: Settings → Edit layout → tap any button to hide it. Start with the buttons most relevant to your child's daily life. You can restore any at any time.

Step 2 — Add personal buttons: Add custom buttons for family members, favourite foods, activities (Premium).

Step 3 — Lock the app: Once set up, enable Carer lock or Kiosk mode so your child can't accidentally change anything (Premium).

Step 4 — Use Search: Teach your child to tap 🔍 when they can't find a button — it searches everything instantly.
⭐ Custom buttons PREMIUM
In edit mode, tap any + ADD slot → pick an emoji or upload a photo → type the label → choose a colour → Save ✓.

Custom buttons appear seamlessly after built-ins — no gap, no new row.

Premium: 29 extra slots per category

🎨 Button colours PREMIUM
Three ways to change colours:

1. Single button — pick a colour swatch when adding or editing a custom button.
2. Whole category — Settings → Display → Button Colours → pick a colour under any category.
3. All at once — same section → 🪣 Apply one colour to all → one tap changes everything.

Tap ↺ Reset to restore the original pastel colours.
🌍 Customise buttons & phrases PREMIUM
Settings → Customise → Edit top buttons.

Change the label and spoken phrase for the 5 top buttons and all 6 emergency phrases — in any language.

Example: I AM → मैं हूँ · I WANT → मुझे चाहिए · HELP ME → مدد کرو
📅 Visual Schedule PREMIUM
Tap 📅 in the top bar. PIN protected — only carers can edit.

To set up: tap ✏️ Edit → pick emoji or photo → type activity → add time → tap Add ✓.

Daily use: child taps each card to tick it off ✓ (turns green). Next activity highlighted in blue. Tap Reset ticks each morning.

Helps reduce anxiety around transitions.
🎙️ Voice recording PREMIUM
Record your own voice for any button in any language.

Custom buttons: Edit mode → tap the button → select Record voice
Built-in buttons: Edit mode → hold button 1 second → tap Cancel on delete prompt → voice record opens

Tap 🎙️, speak clearly (max 3 seconds), tap ▶ Preview then Save ✓.
🔒 Carer lock & Kiosk mode PREMIUM
Carer lock: Settings → Lock with PIN → set a 4-digit PIN. Child cannot access settings while locked.

Kiosk mode: Fullscreen — child cannot leave the app. A red bar appears at the top while active.

To exit kiosk mode:
1. Tap the red bar at the top → enter your PIN
2. Or tap the Aidova logo 7 times → enter your PIN

If you have forgotten your PIN, please contact hello@aidova.app for support.

Tap Done ✓ when finished. All changes saved automatically.

A quick tip before you start
🔍 Can't find a button?
Tap the magnifying glass 🔍 at the top — search works across all 140 buttons instantly.
👁️ Too many buttons?
Go to Settings → Edit layout to hide any buttons your child doesn't need. Less clutter = easier to use. You can always restore them later.
➕ Need a button that's missing?
Settings → Edit layout → tap + ADD to create a custom button with any emoji, photo, or label.
🔒 Handing the device to your child?
Settings → Lock with PIN so they can't change anything. Or use Kiosk mode to lock them fully into the app. To exit kiosk, tap the red bar at the top or tap the logo 7 times.
🆘 Set up the emergency button
Settings → Emergency Contact → add your phone number. If your child holds HELP ME for 2 seconds it will call you.
