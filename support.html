❓ Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Open Aidova and tap any button — it speaks aloud immediately. No account or setup required.

I subscribed but Premium isn't showing

On iOS: Premium activates automatically through your Apple ID. Try closing and reopening the app.
On Android or web: Premium activates using the licence key sent to your email when you subscribed. Go to Settings → Plans → "Have a code?" and enter it. If it still isn't showing, email us and we'll help right away.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

On iOS: Open Aidova on your new device, go to Settings → Plans, and tap "Restore Purchases."
On Android or web: Go to Settings → Plans → "Have a code?" and enter your licence key. Lost your key? Tap "Resend my key" and enter the email you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iOS: Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad to manage or cancel.
On Android or web: Go to Settings → Plans & Upgrade → Manage / Cancel Subscription, or email hello@aidova.app and we'll help directly.

Does Aidova work offline?

Yes. Once opened, Aidova works completely offline — no internet connection needed.

How do I export or print my child's board?

This is a Premium feature. Go to Settings → Print / Export Board → tap "Export current category as PDF" to generate a printable PDF of the current board, handy for school or hospital visits.

How do I see which words my child has been using?

There are two carer tools for this:

Recent Words (Premium) — Settings → Recent Words. Shows the last 30 buttons tapped with a count of how many times each was used this week. Great for spotting which words to promote to permanent custom buttons or sharing with a speech therapist.

Insights (Premium Plus) — Settings → Insights. Shows the top 10 most-used words from the last 7 days, ranked by frequency — a more analytical view for tracking progress over time.

All data stays entirely on your device and is never sent anywhere.

What are Usage Insights and Smart Suggestions?

These are Premium Plus features. Insights (Settings → Insights) shows the most-used words from the last 7 days, helpful for spotting patterns or sharing with an SLT. Smart Suggestions gently prompt you in Edit layout mode to add a frequently-used word as a permanent button. Both stay entirely on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Is my data private?

Yes. All your data stays on your device. We don't collect or store personal communication data.
