Why Aidova exists

A close friend of mine has a non-verbal child.

Over the years I watched the small frustrations that most people never think about. Not being able to say they were hungry. Needing help but not knowing how to ask. Wanting something as simple as a hug and having no easy way to express it.

Their family tried different communication tools. Some were expensive. Some were complicated. Some worked well in certain situations, but they often felt overwhelming for everyday use.

The more I saw, the more I wondered whether communication could be made simpler. Not simpler in a clinical sense. Simpler in a human sense. Something a child, teenager, parent, grandparent, teacher or carer could pick up and understand straight away.

That's where Aidova started. A communication app built around familiar emojis, simple language and personalisation. No training required. No complicated setup. Just a way for someone to be understood.

Aidova continues to grow through conversations with families, carers, teachers and therapists who use it every day. Every suggestion, every story and every piece of feedback helps shape what comes next.

Every button, colour, language, and layout is yours to decide. The app adapts to the person using it — not the other way around.

Accessibility

Built for every visual need and every age

High contrast mode: pure black background, vivid borders — maximum clarity for visual sensitivity. Adult mode: deep professional teal with square buttons, for teens and adults who don't want a child-themed app. Dark mode: easier at night and gentler for light-sensitive users. Button size goes from Small to Large for easier tapping.
