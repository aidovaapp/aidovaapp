FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need internet to use it?
No. Once opened or saved to your home screen, Aidova works 100% offline — anywhere. In the car, at school, on holiday.
Is my data safe?
Yes. No account is ever required, and nothing leaves your phone or tablet. All data stays entirely on your device. Fully UK GDPR compliant.
Can I use it in our home language?
Yes — Premium and Premium Plus let you change every button phrase to any language. You can also record your own voice saying each phrase.
Can our whole family or class use it?
Yes. Premium Plus has unlimited profiles — everyone has their own setup on a single device. Each profile has its own buttons, layout, language and colours.
What if I don't want to pay later?
The Free plan is yours forever — no time limit, no features taken away. You only upgrade if you need custom buttons, voice recording, or multiple profiles.
Is it suitable for adults, not just children?
Absolutely. Adult mode transforms the entire app — professional teal, square buttons, clean design. Used by adults recovering from stroke, people with dementia, and care settings across the UK.
Why Aidova exists

A close friend of mine has a child who doesn't always have the words to say what they need.

Over the years I saw how difficult everyday communication could be. Not big things. Small things — saying "I'm hungry", "I'm scared", "I need help", or simply asking for a hug.

Their family tried different tools. Physical cards that got lost. Apps locked to one device. Tools that felt expensive, complicated, or hard to use every day. Often the person who needed it most wasn't the one setting it up.

Communication tools were often designed for therapy rooms — not real life. But real life doesn't wait for setup or training. It happens in the kitchen. In the car. At school. In care homes. In hospitals.

Most of us say "I'm tired" or "I need help" without thinking. For some people, getting those words out isn't simple at all.

While the journey started with a child, the more people I spoke to, the more I realised the challenge was much bigger. Adults recovering from strokes. People living with cerebral palsy. Older adults affected by dementia. Different situations, but often the same need: a simple way to express themselves and be understood.

Aidova started with a simple question:

What if communication could be easier?
Not easier for therapists. Not easier for technology.
Easier for the person trying to be understood.

Today Aidova is shaped by the people who use it — families, carers, teachers and therapists. Every conversation helps make it a little better.
