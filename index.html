Personalisation

Make it yours

Aidova isn't a one-size-fits-all app. Every button, colour, language, and layout is yours to decide. The app is entirely how you choose to use it.

Aidova was created after seeing first-hand how a friend struggled with their non-verbal child — watching them frustrated, unable to express the simplest things we take for granted. A hug. Being hungry. Feeling scared.

Simple things can make a huge difference. That's why Aidova is built around maximum flexibility and minimum complication — so it works for a child, a teenager, or an adult, exactly the way they need it.

Emojis were chosen because they're universal. No language barriers. No sign language to learn. No symbols only specialists understand. And if you'd rather use photos of real people and real places? Add them. The app adapts to the person using it — not the other way around.

Edit mode

Hide, reorder, and take full control

Too many buttons on screen? Hide the ones you don't need yet. Put the most important ones first. In edit mode, tap any button to hide it, hold to delete it, or drag to reorder. Your child sees exactly what they need — nothing more.

Custom buttons

Add any button, in any language, with any photo

Choose from hundreds of built-in emojis, or upload a photo straight from your camera roll — a family member, a favourite toy, a real place they know. Set the phrase in any language in the world. Press save. Done.

Switch on high contrast mode for a bold black background with vivid colours — easier to see and lower on visual noise. Adult mode swaps the child theme for a deep professional teal — designed for teenagers and adults who want something that feels grown-up.

Why Aidova exists

Born from a real moment

💬

Aidova was created after seeing first-hand how a friend struggled with their non-verbal child — watching them frustrated, unable to express the simplest things. Things we take for granted every day. A hug. Being hungry. Feeling scared.

It became clear that something simple, something anyone could pick up without training or technical knowledge, could make a real difference. Not just for children — but for teenagers, adults, anyone who finds spoken communication difficult.

That's why Aidova uses emojis. They are universal. No language barriers. No sign language to learn. No symbols that only specialists understand. Just tap, and be heard.
