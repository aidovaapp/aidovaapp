💬 How the app works
Tap any button — the app speaks the word out loud. Build sentences by tapping multiple buttons. The green sentence bar at the top shows your words building up. Tap ▶ Speak to say the full sentence — it clears automatically ready for the next one. Tap ✕ to clear manually.
🔝 The 5 top buttons 🆓 FREE
🧍 I AM — how I am feeling right now
🙋 I WANT — something I need or want
✅ YES — agree (with confetti!)
❌ NO — disagree or refuse
🆘 HELP ME — see Emergency section below
🆘 HELP ME button 🆓 FREE
Quick tap → speaks "I need help" twice + screen flashes red
Hold 2 seconds → speaks 3 times + flash + opens the Emergency Screen

Emergency Screen has 6 big buttons:
🆘 I Need Help · 🤕 I Am Hurt · 🏥 I Need Doctor
🚨 I Am Lost · 💧 I Need Water · 📞 Call My Carer

Each speaks loudly at full volume. Call My Carer dials automatically.
To set up the call: Settings → Emergency Contact → enter carer's number.
📂 4 categories 🆓 FREE
Swipe left/right or tap the tabs to move between:
😊 Feelings · 🍎 Needs · 🏠 Places · 🤝 Social
21 buttons in each category (84 total). Scroll down to see your custom buttons below.
🔍 Search 🆓 FREE
Tap 🔍 in the top bar. Type any word — results appear instantly from all 48 built-in buttons and your custom buttons. Tap ▶ Speak to hear it, or tap the result to jump straight to that category.
🧘 Calm Breathing 🆓 FREE
Tap 🧘 in the top bar. A large circle expands and shrinks — child follows it to calm down.

Pattern: Breathe in 4s → Hold 4s → Breathe out 6s
Circle turns teal (breathe in), gold (hold), blue (breathe out). Counts completed cycles. Great for anxiety, meltdowns or overwhelm.
⏱️ Visual Timer 🆓 FREE
Tap ⏱️ in the top bar. Choose 1, 2, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes. A green ring counts down visually — turns orange then red as time runs out. Says "Time is up!" and flashes when finished.

Great for transitions: "5 more minutes then bath time."
🌙 Dark mode 🆓 FREE
Settings → Dark mode toggle. Dark navy background — easier on the eyes at night or for light-sensitive users. Setting is remembered between sessions.
🧑 Adult mode 🆓 FREE
Settings → Adult mode toggle. Switches to a deep teal professional theme with rounded square buttons instead of circles. Designed for teenagers, adults and anyone who finds the default child-friendly colours too young. Can be combined with dark mode.
🌍 Customise buttons & phrases PREMIUM
Settings → 🌍 Customise → Edit top buttons.

Change the label and spoken phrase for any of the 5 top buttons and all 6 emergency phrases — in any language.

For example a Hindi-speaking family can change:
• I AM → मैं हूँ
• I WANT → मुझे चाहिए
• HELP ME → मदद करो
• Emergency: I AM HURT → मुझे चोट लगी है

The button shows your new label and speaks your new phrase when tapped. Tap Reset to English to restore defaults at any time.
🔊 Voice 🆓 FREE
Settings → Voice → tap 👩 Female or 👨 Male → tap ▶ Test to hear it.

For the clearest British voice: iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English → download Serena (female) or Daniel (male). These are free to download and sound much more natural.
👤 Profiles 🆓 FREE (2 profiles)
Settings → Profiles. Switch between up to 2 profiles on the free plan (unlimited with Premium). Each profile has its own custom buttons. Tap a profile to switch — great if multiple children share the same device.
⭐ Custom buttons PREMIUM
Scroll to the bottom of any category → tap + ADD

• Pick an emoji from the list (family, food, sensory, activities etc)
• OR upload a photo from your camera roll (e.g. a photo of Grandma)
• Type the button label (e.g. GRANDMA, BUBBLES)
• Tap Save ✓

Premium: 29 custom slots per category — 200 buttons total
Premium Plus: 54 custom slots per category — 300 buttons total

Empty slots are hidden in normal use — they only appear in Edit mode.
📅 Visual Schedule PREMIUM
Tap 📅 in the top bar.

Carer sets it up (once):
Tap ✏️ Edit → tap the 📌 icon to pick an emoji → type the activity name → add optional time → tap Add ✓. Repeat for whole day (Breakfast, School, Bath etc).

Child uses it daily:
Big colourful cards — tap any card to tick it off ✓ (turns green). The next activity is highlighted in blue so they always know what's coming next. Tap Reset ticks each morning to start fresh.

🔒 Edit and Reset are PIN protected — child can only tick activities off, not change or delete them.

Helps reduce anxiety around transitions.
🎙️ Record your own voice PREMIUM
Record your voice for any button in any language — English, Hindi, Urdu, Arabic, Polish, anything.

1. Settings → Edit layout → Edit
2. Hold any button for 1 second
3. For custom buttons: type 1 to record voice
4. Tap 🎙️ and speak clearly (max 3 seconds)
5. Tap ▶ Preview to check it
6. Tap Save ✓

Works on ALL buttons — built-in (Happy, Toilet etc) and custom.
✏️ Edit layout PREMIUM PLUS
Settings → Edit layout → Edit. Orange bar appears at bottom.

In edit mode you can:
• Tap any button to hide it (fades) or show it again
• Hold 1 second → orange ▲▼◀▶ arrows appear outside the circle — tap to move the button
• Hold a custom button 1s → type 1=Record voice · 2=Edit · 3=Delete

Tap Done ✓ when finished. Hidden buttons stay hidden until you go back into edit mode.
🔒 Carer lock PREMIUM
Settings → Lock with PIN → choose a 4-digit PIN. Child cannot access settings while locked.

Forgot your PIN?
Enter the wrong PIN twice → tap "Forgot PIN? Tap here" → type: CHEWAIDRESET
PIN is cleared — all your buttons and settings stay safe.

Alternative: tap the Aidova logo 7 times to access the forgot PIN option.
📺 Kiosk mode PREMIUM
Settings → Kiosk mode. App goes fullscreen and the back button is disabled — child cannot leave the app accidentally. Requires PIN to exit. Ideal for school or respite settings.
📝 Sentence bar 🆓 FREE
The green bar below the top buttons builds your sentence as you tap. Tap ▶ Speak to say the full sentence — clears automatically after. Tap ✕ to clear manually at any time.
🚨 Emergency screen 🆓 FREE
Hold HELP ME for 2 seconds → full red emergency screen opens with 6 big buttons:

🆘 I Need Help · 🤕 I Am Hurt · 🏥 I Need Doctor
🚨 I Am Lost · 💧 I Need Water · 📞 Call My Carer

Each speaks at full volume. Call My Carer dials your carer's number automatically.
Setup: Settings → Emergency Contact → enter carer's mobile number.

⚠️ Important: Keep iPhone volume turned up using the side buttons for the loudest sound in emergencies.
⚡ High contrast 🆓 FREE
Settings → Display → High contrast toggle.
Black background with vivid coloured borders — maximum visibility for visual impairment or bright sunlight. Overrides dark and adult modes. Setting is saved permanently.
📏 Button size 🆓 FREE
Settings → Display → Button size slider.

• Small — more compact, fits more on screen
• Normal — default size
• Large — 30% bigger, easier to tap for visual impairment or motor difficulties. Swipe sideways if buttons extend off screen.
🔊 Volume control 🆓 FREE
Settings → Voice → Volume slider. Drag from 0% to 100%.

💡 For maximum volume: Use the iPhone side buttons to turn up the device volume first — the app cannot override the hardware volume.
