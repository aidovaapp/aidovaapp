FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need internet to use it?
No. Once opened or added to your home screen, Aidova works fully offline — in the car, at school, on holiday.
Is my data safe?
Yes. Everything stays on your device. No account is required and nothing is sent anywhere.
Can I use it in our home language?
Yes. Premium plans let you change every button phrase to any language, and record your own voice saying each one.
Can our whole family or class use it?
Yes. Premium Plus supports unlimited profiles across up to 5 devices — each profile with their own buttons, layout, and settings.
What if I don't want to pay later?
The Free plan has no time limit and no features taken away. Upgrade only if you need voice recording, custom buttons, or multiple profiles.
Do I need to download anything?
Aidova works instantly in your browser — no download needed. You can also add it to your home screen on iPhone, iPad and Android, where it works just like a native app, even offline. Available on the App Store and Google Play.
Does Aidova replace speech therapy?
No. Aidova is a communication tool designed to support everyday communication. Many families and therapists use it alongside speech and language therapy — not instead of it.
Can I use Aidova on more than one device?
Yes. Aidova works on any phone or tablet — iPhone, iPad, Android. Open it in a browser on any device, or add it to the home screen. Each device stores its own settings locally.
How does Premium work across multiple devices?
When you subscribe, we email you a personal licence key (e.g. AID-7X9K-2M4P-8Q3R). Enter this key on any device to activate Premium — it works on as many devices as you need. If you lose your key, tap "Resend my key" on the Plans screen and we'll send it straight to your email.

Why Aidova exists

Aidova began with a child who didn't always have the words to say what they needed.

I watched the people around them try their best to understand. Sometimes it was obvious. Often it wasn't.

The hardest moments weren't the big ones. They were the everyday moments most of us take for granted.

Wanting a drink.
Feeling scared.
Needing help.
Asking for a hug.
Saying "I'm tired."
Saying "I love you."

Simple things that should be easy to say, but sometimes couldn't be.

Like many families, they tried different communication tools. Some relied on physical cards that got lost or left behind. Some apps felt complicated, expensive, or tied to a single device. Many seemed designed for clinics and therapy rooms rather than kitchens, playgrounds, classrooms, cars, and everyday life.

What stood out most wasn't the technology. It was the frustration.

The frustration of having something important to say and not being able to say it.
The frustration of the people who cared deeply and wanted to help, but couldn't always understand.

Over time, I realised this wasn't just one family's experience. I spoke with parents of autistic children. Adults rebuilding their lives after a stroke. People living with cerebral palsy. Families supporting loved ones with dementia. Different stories, different circumstances, but often the same challenge: the need to be understood.

Because communication is about far more than words.

Independence.
Choice.
Comfort.
Connection.
Dignity.

Aidova started with a simple question:

What if communication could be easier?
Not easier for technology. Not easier for systems.
Easier for the person trying to be understood.

Today, Aidova continues to be shaped by the people who use it every day — families, carers, teachers, therapists, and individuals finding their own voice in different ways. Since then, we've spoken with parents, carers, teachers, therapists and adults with communication challenges to shape every part of Aidova. Every conversation teaches us something. Every piece of feedback helps us improve. And every time someone can say what they need, how they feel, or who they love — we're reminded why Aidova exists.
