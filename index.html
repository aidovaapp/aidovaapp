❓ Quick Guide
💬 How the app works
Tap any button — the app speaks the word out loud. Build sentences by tapping multiple buttons. The sentence bar shows your words. Tap ▶ Speak to say the full sentence — it clears automatically after speaking.
🔝 The 5 top buttons
I AM · I WANT · YES (confetti!) · NO · 🆘 HELP ME

Quick tap HELP ME = speaks "I need help" + red flash
Hold 2 seconds HELP ME = speaks + flash + sends emergency SMS to carer
📝 Sentence bar
Builds your sentence as you tap. Tap ▶ Speak to say it all — clears automatically. Tap ✕ to clear manually.
⏱️ Visual Timer
Tap ⏱️ in the top bar. Choose 1, 2, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes. A green ring counts down visually — turns orange then red as time runs out. When finished it flashes and says "Time is up!" Great for transitions like "5 more minutes then bath time".
🔍 Search
Tap 🔍 top right → type any word → results appear instantly. Tap ▶ Speak or tap a result to jump to that category.
📂 The 4 categories
Swipe left/right or tap tabs: 😊 Feelings · 🍎 Needs · 🏠 Places · 🤝 Social. Each has 12 buttons. Scroll down for custom buttons.
🌙 Dark mode
Settings → Dark mode toggle. Dark background — easier on the eyes at night or for light-sensitive users.
🔊 Voice
Settings → Voice → 👩 Female or 👨 Male → ▶ Test.
For clearest British voice: iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English → download Serena or Daniel.
🆘 Emergency SMS
Settings → Emergency Contact → set carer's number. Hold HELP ME 2 seconds → sends SMS automatically with child's name.
⭐ Custom buttons (Premium)
Scroll to bottom of any category → tap + ADD → pick emoji or photo → type label → Save ✓. Empty slots hidden in normal use.
✏️ Edit mode (Premium Plus)
Settings → Edit layout. Then:
• Tap button to hide/show
• Hold 1s → ▲▼◀▶ arrows appear — tap to move
• Hold custom button 1s → 1=Record voice · 2=Edit · 3=Delete
Tap Done ✓ when finished.
🎙️ Record voice (Premium)
Any language — Hindi, Urdu, Arabic, Polish, anything.
1. Settings → Edit layout → Edit
2. Hold any button 1 second
3. Tap 🎙️ → speak (max 3s) → Save ✓
🔒 Carer lock & Forgot PIN
Settings → Lock with PIN. Forgot it? Enter wrong PIN twice → tap "Forgot PIN?" → type CHEWAIDRESET. All data stays safe.
OR tap Aidova logo 7 times.
📺 Kiosk mode
Settings → Kiosk mode. Fullscreen, back button disabled. PIN to exit.
👤 Profiles
Settings → Profiles. Up to 3 — one per child. Each has their own custom buttons.
