Personalisation

Make it yours

Every button, colour, language, and layout is yours to decide. The app is entirely how you choose to use it.

Edit mode

Hide, reorder, and control every button

Enter edit mode and every button gets a dashed border. Tap once to hide it, hold to delete it. Tap Reorder to drag buttons into exactly the order you want — most-used at the top. Tapping any button opens its options: record your own voice, edit the label, hide it, or delete it permanently.

👁️ Tap to hide ☰ Drag to reorder 🗑️ Hold to delete 🎙️ Record your voice ✏️ Edit any label
Any language · Any photo · Your voice

Fully personalised — in any language, with any photo

Change any button phrase to any language. Record your own voice — a parent's voice saying the words in the child's home language is far more meaningful than a computer. Add custom buttons with hundreds of built-in emojis or upload your own photos — a family member, a favourite food, a real place they know.

🌍 Any language 🎙️ Record your voice 📷 Upload photos ⭐ Hundreds of emojis 🎨 Pick a colour
High contrast mode

Black background, vivid colours — maximum clarity

One toggle switches the whole app to a pure black background with bold, vivid circle borders. Emojis pop, text is bright white, visual clutter disappears. Ideal for users with visual sensitivity, sensory processing differences, or anyone who finds high contrast easier to use.

⚡ Pure black background 🔴 Vivid colour borders 👁️ Reduced visual noise
Adult mode

A grown-up look for teens and adults

The default theme uses round circles and warm colours designed for children. Switch on Adult mode and the whole app transforms — deep professional teal, square buttons, clean typography. Same communication tools, completely different feel. One toggle in settings.

🧑 Deep teal theme 🟦 Square button style 🔀 One toggle to switch
Dark mode & button size

Easier on the eyes, sized just right

Dark mode switches to a deep navy background with warm-toned buttons — easier at night and less intense for light-sensitive users. The button size slider goes from Small to Large, useful for younger children or those with motor difficulties.

🌙 Deep navy background 😌 Easier at night 🔠 Small to Large size

Why Aidova exists

Born from a real moment

💬

Aidova was created after seeing first-hand how a friend struggled with their non-verbal child — watching them frustrated, unable to express the simplest things. Things we take for granted every day. A hug. Being hungry. Feeling scared.

It became clear that something simple, something anyone could pick up without training or technical knowledge, could make a real difference. Not just for children — but for teenagers, adults, anyone who finds spoken communication difficult.

That's why Aidova uses emojis. They are universal. No language barriers. No sign language to learn. No symbols that only specialists understand. Just tap, and be heard.

🎯

Help every person communicate — child, teenager or adult — with as much flexibility and as little complication as possible.
