Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 2025  ·  JMC Collective Ltd  ·  England, UK

Aidova is an AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication) app designed from the ground up for people who find communication difficult. Accessibility is not an afterthought — it is the entire purpose of the app.

Our Commitment

Aidova is committed to making communication accessible to everyone — regardless of age, ability, language, or device. We design every feature with the user's needs first, not the technology.

We actively seek feedback from families, carers, teachers, speech and language therapists, and adults who use AAC to continuously improve the accessibility of the app.

Accessibility Features

🔤 Large touch targets

All communication buttons are large, well-spaced, and easy to tap — even for users with limited motor control.

⚡ High contrast mode

Black background with vivid colour borders for maximum visibility. Designed for users with visual sensitivity.

🔠 Adjustable button size

Small, Normal and Large button sizes to suit different users and screen sizes.

🌙 Dark mode

Reduces eye strain, especially for night-time use or users with light sensitivity.

🧑 Adult mode

Professional teal theme with square buttons — designed for teenagers and adults. Avoids childish aesthetics for adult users.

🔊 Text-to-speech

All buttons speak aloud using clear, natural voices. Male and female voice options available.

🎙️ Voice recording

Record a familiar voice for any button — in any language. Particularly valuable for children who respond better to a parent's voice.

🌍 Any language

All button phrases can be customised to any language, supporting multilingual families and communities.

📴 Works offline

No internet connection required. Works in hospitals, schools, cars, and anywhere else — reducing barriers to access.

🧘 Calm breathing tool

Animated breathing guide to support emotional regulation during overwhelming moments or meltdowns.

⏱️ Visual timer

Clear visual countdown to support transitions — particularly helpful for autistic users who need predictability.

📅 Visual schedule

Daily schedule with emoji and photo support to reduce anxiety around routine and transitions.

Designed For

  • Non-speaking and minimally speaking individuals
  • Autistic children and adults
  • People with speech delays or disorders
  • Adults recovering from stroke or living with aphasia
  • People living with cerebral palsy
  • People living with dementia or cognitive decline
  • People with selective mutism
  • Anyone who finds verbal communication difficult in some situations

Schools and Professional Settings

Aidova is used in special schools, mainstream schools with SEN units, speech and language therapy sessions, occupational therapy, care homes, and day centres.

  • Unlimited profiles allow one device to support an entire classroom
  • Carer lock and Kiosk mode allow safe, supervised use
  • Each profile has its own layout, language and button settings
  • No account required — instant setup in any session

Technical Accessibility

  • Works as a Progressive Web App (PWA) — installable on any device without an app store
  • Responsive design — works on phones, tablets and desktop browsers
  • Touch-optimised — designed for touchscreen use
  • No cookies or tracking — privacy by design
  • All data stored locally on device — no server dependency

Known Limitations

We are continuously improving Aidova. Current known limitations include:

  • Screen reader (VoiceOver/TalkBack) support is limited — the app is primarily designed for direct touch interaction
  • Switch access is not currently supported
  • Eye gaze technology is not currently supported

If you require any of these features, please contact us — user needs directly shape our development roadmap.

⚕️ Medical disclaimer: Aidova is a communication support tool and is not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, monitor or prevent any medical condition. It is not a replacement for speech and language therapy or professional intervention.
